Health and Wellness Resource (HWR) Website

June - July 2018

This project was assigned to me after the business department of Morneau Shepell wanted to digitizing their health and wellness resource (HWR) kits, a resource packages aimed to guide people through 8 different topics such as, "Enhancing Your Relationships", "Separation & Divorce", and "Eating for your Health." The kits contain books, CDs, pamphlets, and a workbook with content, quizzes and exercises. By transitioning the kits to an online medium, we can create a dynamic, easy-to-use product for the user, as well as save the costs from assembling and shipping the kits to each customer.


Due to the existence of the workbook and pamphlets, the design was inspired by magazine layouts, note the text in columns, the illustrations, and the numbered menu like chapters in a book. To replace the book included in each package, a $5 Amazon gift card for an e-book was included instead.


*DISCLAIMER*: the illustrations featured in my designs are from Dutch artist Maya Stepien and her work for Google Calendar and Google's Design Sprint Kit. They were used as placeholders to accurately portray the artistic direction of this project.

UI Design Decisions

  • On the list of articles shown, a small description was included with the image and title. Based on user testing, this was prefered over an image and title without the text with the number one indicated reasons that users wanted a quick summary of the article they'd be clicking.
  • "Article", "Quiz", and "Exercise" were replaced with "Learn", "Think", and "Do", respectively, to add a unique twist to the otherwise frequently used key-words.
  • "Learn", "Think", and "Do" are color coded with Morneau Shepell brand colors to visually group each topic.
  • There are 2 versions of the article layout, left-aligned and right-aligned. This is to provide some dimensional contrast to the user.
  • A survey is included on the resource page so we can receive feedback about the website.
  • WCAG 2.0 color compliant.
