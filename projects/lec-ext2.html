Proposition to Rebuild: Cat's Eye Bridge

November 2017

Not real unicorns. In the financial world, a unicorn is a company valued at over $1 billion, and the name is fitting because like the mythical unicorn, these companies are so rare to find.


This report utilized Phase 4 of my Design Process as I gathered data from the 4 highest evaluated unicorn companies, Uber, Xiaomi, Airbnb and Didi Chuxing. I found that the time it took for the companies to become unicorns vary from 1 to 4 years after being founded. As well, Didi Chuxing shows that you do not need to serve the entire world to become a unicorn, as they only operate in China.


This topic was inspired by Angela Tran Kingyen's lecture at ESEC (Engineering Science Education Conference), an annual conference held at UofT for Engineering Science students led by past Engsci alumnis. Angela's talk was very captivating and educating; she taught a lot about the business, investment, and venture capitalist world in her short presentation. She especially caught my attention when she introduced unicorns, leading me to do research on my own and discover companies that I never knew existed.
