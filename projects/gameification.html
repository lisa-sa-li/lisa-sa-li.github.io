My Migo - Positive Play

June - August 2017

View My Prototype

Mobile

Morneau Shepell's My Migo program aims to help users set and achieve health and wellness goals through features offered by the app, such as online therapy, breathing exercises, goal-achievements, and more. An additional feature to the app was Positive Play, a set of achievement badges to "gamify" My Migo, meaning adding game-elements to non-game contexts which has been shown to improve user engagement, learning, physical exercise, and more.


The set of badges I created and designed were meant to encourage:

  • Health through means of diet, sleep, and exercise
  • Relationships with friends, family, and colleagues
  • Setting and completing more goals
  • Use of other features in the app

Info Flow

UI Design Decisions

  • Each badge has 3 tiers (denoted as stars) encourage the user to accomplish their goal and eventually form actions into habits
    • Completed tiers are colored while uncompleted remain in black and white
    • Star graphics double as a progress bar and fill up in accordance to number of times goal has been achieved
  • User can toggle between Badges and My Goal, which displays their initial goal and offers the inspiration graphic of a rocket that symbolizes their increasing momentum to achieving their objective
  • Prominent appearance and popup on dashboard to foster a goal-oriented environment
