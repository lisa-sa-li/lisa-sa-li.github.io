iCBT Landing Page and Self-Register

June 2018

Cognitive Behaviour Therapy (CBT) is the most widely used evidence-based practice for improving mental health. It focuses on the development of personal coping strategies and skills that target solving current problems and changing unhelpful patterns of thought, beliefs, attitudes, and behaviours. iCBT is Cognitive Behaviour Therapy through a digital platform.


Morneau Shepell's iCBT program is CBT that is delivered by a therapist through an online platform. Combining the convenience of online access with the same benefits of in-person Cognitive Behaviour Therapy, iCBT targets gaps in treatment, which in turn reduces absenteeism and shortens return to work time frames.[1]
