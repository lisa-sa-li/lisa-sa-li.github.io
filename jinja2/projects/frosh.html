The University of Toronto F!rosh Week 1T8 (2018) is the Faculty of Engineering's official student-run orientation week. As Canada's oldest engineering school, there's a rich history of unique events, traditions, and school spirit which we introduce to to help orient and welcome the incoming first years. So why is the exclamation mark in the word F!rosh? Everyone's so excited that even the exclamation couldn't wait until the end of the word.


Under the Marketing Committee, I was the chair of the Graphics sub-committee, along with my girl Karen Zhao. For the duration of the summer, we simultaneously worked on projects for other subcommittees to create designs to reach a particular audience.


Responsibilities

  • Logo and branding for the 2018 year and theme: SPY!
  • T-shirt designs for the incoming Frosh, executives, subcommittee chairs, F!rosh Week leadurs (purposely spelled "leedur"), first aid team (Skule Patrol), photography and video.
  • Flyers and posters for some of the 18 events over 7 days.
  • Merchandise designs for some of the 19 (20?) subcommittees.
  • Marketable content for social media for Facebook, Snapchat, and Instagram.
  • Attend bi-weekly briefings to update the committee on our ongoing projects and progress.
