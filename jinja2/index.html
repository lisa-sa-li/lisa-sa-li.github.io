Who's this??

I'm an engineering student by day, and a slightly more stressed engineering student by night. As an Engineering Science student at the University of Toronto, with extensive experience in UX design and software engineering, I look for solutions that are rooted in usability, aesthetics, and innovation.

What I Design For

My goal is to design user interfaces that anyone can use without much effort. This does not mean restricting myself to incredibly simplistic designs; as Steve Krug, a usability expert, puts it: using new conventions is acceptable if it "adds so much value that it's worth a small learning curve".
